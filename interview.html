Interview Script

1 Opening Question

{The first question should always be "How was it?", an open question to encourage the user to speak freely.}

  • “How was it?”

    Let the user speak, and encourage them, until they dry up and stop of their own accord.



2 Standard Questions

{Next, ask a series of standard questions prepared in advance for every test user. More general questions should come first, more specific questions later. At least four standard questions should be asked in this section. Two are already suggested, add at least two more of your own.}

  1. “Did anything strike you as particularly good?”

  2. “Did anything strike you as particularly bad?”

  3. “How did you find the navigation and search functionality of the site?”

  4. “Would you consider using alza.at for future electronics purchases? Why or why not?”

3 Individual Questions

At this point, ask any questions which arose during the test with each particular test user.
